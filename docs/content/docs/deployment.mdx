---
title: Deployment
description: Graceful shutdown on SIGINT and SIGTERM, and running in containers.
---

`athena serve` and `athena telegram` stop the same way on SIGINT (Ctrl-C)
and SIGTERM (`kill`, `docker stop`, systemd): they take no new work, let the
turns in flight finish and reply, then exit 0.

## Containers

Run `athena` directly: exec-form `CMD ["athena", "serve"]`, or
`docker run --init`. A shell wrapper as PID 1 does not pass SIGTERM on.

## Grace periods

Stopping takes as long as the slowest turn in flight, and for the bot up to
one more long poll (10 s). Docker waits 10 s and systemd 90 s before SIGKILL,
which cannot be caught and loses those turns. Raise the grace period to
cover a slow tool-using turn:

- `docker stop -t`
- `stop_grace_period` in Compose
- `TimeoutStopSec` in systemd

A second signal quits `serve` at once, and the turns in flight are lost as
in a crash. The bot ignores a second signal, and ignores a signal that
arrives before it starts polling.

<Callout type="warn">
  Neither transport has authentication. Keep `serve` on loopback or put an
  authenticating proxy in front. See [Known limits](/docs/known-limits).
</Callout>
